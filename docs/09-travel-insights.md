# Insight #002

## Date

2026-08-05

## Location

Amed, Bali

## Situation

While travelling I needed several things within a short period of time:

- ATM
- Powerbank
- Waterproof waist bag
- Diving school
- Action camera

## Observation

Finding local shops, comparing products and understanding local offers in a foreign country took much more time than expected.

I used ChatGPT several times to:

- find nearby shops
- compare products
- evaluate prices
- choose between different options

## Problem

Travellers often don't know:

- where to buy something
- which brands are trustworthy
- whether prices are fair
- which option best fits their situation

Information is spread across Google Maps, online shops, Reddit, blogs and local recommendations.

## Opportunity

Nomad Guide could become a single place that helps travellers:

- find nearby places
- compare products
- recommend suitable options
- explain local shopping situations
- integrate external services instead of replacing them

## Priority

Future Vision

## Notes

Integrate before reinventing.

The value is not owning the data, but helping travellers make better decisions faster.
